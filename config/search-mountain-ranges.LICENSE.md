Mountain range names and aliases in search-mountain-ranges.json are adapted from the [GeoNames geographical database](https://www.geonames.org/) under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The build script selects named mountain ranges (MTS), groups them by country, lowercases the names and removes duplicates. The index describes names, not geographic boundaries.
